ZeeAcc

Accounting, inventory, payroll and invoicing software for small and medium businesses. Works on Windows and Android. Your books stay on your own device, with optional backup to your own Google Drive.

What it does

Accounting

Chart of accounts, vouchers, ledgers, trial balance, income statement and balance sheet.

Sales & purchases

Invoices, quotations, suppliers, customers, receivables and payables.

Inventory

Items, stock in packets or single units, stock reports and low-stock alerts.

Payroll & HR

Employees, attendance, salary sheets, advances, documents and letters.

Multiple companies

Keep separate books for each company and project.

Sync with Google Drive

Optionally back up and sync your company file to your own Google Drive, between your PC and phone.

About Google Drive sync

Sync is optional. When you turn it on, ZeeAcc asks permission to create and manage only the files it creates itself in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It cannot see your other files. See the privacy policy for details.
