Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how the ZeeAcc application (“ZeeAcc”, “the app”) handles information. ZeeAcc is accounting software that runs on your Windows PC and Android phone.

1. Information the app stores

The accounting data you enter (companies, accounts, vouchers, customers, suppliers, items, employees, attachments and settings) is stored on your own device. We do not run a server that receives or stores your accounting data.

2. Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to turn on sync or backup, the app asks you to sign in with Google and to allow the permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permission lets the app create and access only the files and folders that the app itself creates in your Google Drive (a folder named “Accounts Book Sync” and the company files inside it). The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

  • The sign-in token is kept on your device. It is not sent to us.
  • The company files are sent directly from your device to your own Google Drive and back, so your PC and phone can share the same books.
  • You can stop sync at any time from the app’s settings, and you can remove the app’s access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

3. How Google user data is used

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google Drive is used only to provide the backup and sync features you asked for. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not shared with other people or companies. Humans do not read your data unless you give your explicit permission, for example when you send us a file for support.

4. FBR e-invoicing (optional)

If you turn on FBR digital invoicing (Pakistan), the invoice details are sent directly from your device to the FBR system using the token you enter. We do not receive these details.

5. Other information

The app does not include advertising and does not use analytics or tracking tools. It checks for new app versions by downloading a version file from a public update location; no personal information is sent with this request.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. Your data is shared with a third party only when you choose to use a feature that does so, such as Google Drive sync or FBR e-invoicing, and only with that service.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device and in your own Google Drive until you delete it. To delete it, remove the company in the app, delete the “Accounts Book Sync” folder in your Google Drive, and remove the app’s access in your Google Account. Uninstalling the app removes its local data on that device.

8. Security

Your data is protected by the security of your own device and Google account. Please keep your device, passwords and backups safe.

9. Children

ZeeAcc is business software and is not directed to children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date above.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.
